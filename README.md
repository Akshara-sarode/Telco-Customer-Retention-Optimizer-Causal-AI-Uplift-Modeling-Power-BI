Traditional churn prediction models identify customers who are likely to leave—but they don't answer the most important business question:

> **"Which customers should actually receive a retention offer?"**

This project applies **Causal AI and uplift modeling** to estimate the **Individual Treatment Effect (ITE)** of a retention campaign, enabling marketing teams to focus only on customers who are likely to stay because of the intervention.

The result is a more efficient retention strategy that improves marketing ROI while reducing unnecessary discount spending.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Many organizations waste retention budgets by offering discounts to:

* Customers who would have stayed without incentives
* Customers who will leave regardless of incentives

This creates unnecessary marketing costs and reduces profitability.

This project addresses the problem by identifying the **Persuadable** customer segment—the group most likely to respond positively to a retention offer.

# 🧪 Experimental Framework

The project simulates a Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT):

### Treatment Group

* 50% of customers received a **20% retention discount**

### Control Group

* Remaining customers received no offer

### Objective

Estimate the Individual Treatment Effect:

```text
Uplift = P(Stay | Treatment) − P(Stay | Control)
```

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 🎯 Predictive Churn ≠ Marketing ROI

High churn probability does not necessarily indicate a customer should receive a discount.

---

### 💰 Persuadables Drive Incremental Revenue

The highest marketing ROI comes from customers whose behavior changes because of the retention offer.

---

### 🚫 Avoid Blanket Discounts

Long-term contract customers remain loyal without incentives, making discounts unnecessary.

---

### 📉 Reduce Marketing Waste

Avoid spending retention budgets on customers unlikely to respond.
